# Opcodes the originals dispatch and this repo does not model

The originals were asked directly rather than through a list of ours: the client's message
dispatcher holds three `hash_map<u16, method>` tables (**320** opcodes), and the vSRO
GameServer holds two flat arrays of 2048 entries indexed by `opcode & 0x7FF` (**172**
opcodes, the union of its character and player tables).

Cross-checked both ways:

- The client's 320 match `docs/re/net/original-client-message-map.md` entry for entry — two
  independent routes to the same table, so neither is a transcription accident.
- Two opcodes an earlier string-scan reported as server-only, `0x30B1` and `0xB063`, are
  **artefacts**: the constants never appear in those bodies, the operands are
  function-internal jump targets. The real neighbours are `0x70B1` and `0x7063`.

**241 opcodes below appear in neither** `docs/protocol/opcodes.md` nor its prose. That is the
unexplored surface, not a defect list: an opcode the original dispatches may still be dead in
practice.

### One property worth knowing before reading the list

The server indexes its tables with `opcode & 0x7FF` — **only bit 11 is examined**. So
`0x3010`, `0x7010` and `0xB010` reach the *same* handler there. Direction, in the server's
routing, is not encoded the way our three-space reading of the numbers suggests.
